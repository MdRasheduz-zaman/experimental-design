## ✅ One-page pre-flight checklist

Complete this before the first sample is collected or the analysis starts. Mark
“not applicable” with a reason and a substitute safeguard for observational, descriptive
or predictive work. For example, replace allocation randomization with sampling or
data-split design. A checklist should test your reasoning, not force every study into
a treatment experiment.

**Question**
- [ ] The question is one sentence, and its type (Q1–Q8) is named.
- [ ] The claim I hope to make matches the type.

**Units**
- [ ] Assignment/sampling units, biological sources and independent runs are named separately; counts support the intended claim.
- [ ] Sub-units and clusters are listed and will be aggregated or modelled.

**Allocation**
- [ ] Allocation is randomized with software; the seed is recorded.
- [ ] Processing order, positions and batch membership are randomized or balanced.
- [ ] Known nuisance factors are addressed; complete blocks contain all treatments, or incomplete blocks connect the required comparisons.
- [ ] Allocation is concealed and assessment/analysis blinded where feasible; limitations and substitutes are documented.

**Controls**
- [ ] The comparator isolates the effect of interest (vehicle/sham, not just untreated).
- [ ] Major alternative explanations have controls or explicit limitations; assay capability is checked with a positive/reference control where appropriate.
- [ ] Controls run concurrently and in every batch.

**Size**
- [ ] The size target is justified: meaningful effect, desired precision or predictive performance.
- [ ] Variance/ICC assumptions have sources and sensitivity checks; clustering and attrition rounding are accounted for.
- [ ] The calculation (or simulation) is saved with the protocol.

**Plan**
- [ ] Primary outcome, estimand, model, exclusions, missing-data handling and multiplicity are written down.
- [ ] Stopping/interim rules and feasibility criteria are set before inspecting outcomes.
- [ ] Exploratory analyses are labelled as such.
- [ ] Validation (replication, orthogonal method or external data) is planned.
- [ ] The relevant reporting guideline has been read; the plan is registered if appropriate.
- [ ] Data, code and metadata management are planned (FAIR).
