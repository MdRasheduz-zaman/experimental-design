# Appendix C — Reporting Guidelines by Study Type

> [← Appendix B](A2-design-card-and-checklist.md) · [Table of Contents](../README.md) · [📚 Full reference list](../REFERENCES.md)

Reporting guidelines are most useful **before** an experiment: every item corresponds to a
design decision. Find your study type, read the guideline, and add its items to your
Design Card.

| Study type | Guideline | Design decisions it forces | Course chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Randomized trial — protocol | SPIRIT [@chan2013] | full methods before enrolment | 23, 25 |
| Randomized trial — report | CONSORT 2025 [@hopewell2025]; CONSORT 2010 [@schulz2010] | allocation sequence and concealment, blinding, primary outcome, sample size | 4, 23 |
| Statistical analysis plan | SAP guidance [@gamble2017] | models, covariates, missing data, multiplicity | 25 |
| Trials of AI interventions | CONSORT-AI [@liu2020] | algorithm version, inputs, human–AI interaction | 12, 23 |
| Observational epidemiology | STROBE [@vonelm2007] | study base, confounders, selection | 9, 11 |
| Mendelian randomization | STROBE-MR [@skrivankova2021] | instruments, assumptions, sensitivity analyses | 11 |
| Diagnostic accuracy | STARD 2015 [@bossuyt2015] | reference standard, patient spectrum, blinded reading | 15 |
| Prediction models (incl. ML) | TRIPOD [@collins2015]; TRIPOD+AI [@collins2024] | sample size, data splits, calibration, external validation | 12 |
| Supervised ML in biology | DOME [@walsh2021] | data provenance, split independence, optimization, evaluation | 12, 22 |
| Animal experiments | ARRIVE 2.0 [@percie2020] | unit, randomization, blinding, sample size, inclusion criteria | 23 |
| Livestock & food-safety trials | REFLECT [@sargeant2010] | pen/herd clustering, allocation, outcomes | 19 |
| Experimental pharmacology | BJP guidance [@curtis2018; @curtis2022] | ≥ 5 independent units/group, randomization, blinding, normalization | 18 |
| qPCR/digital PCR | MIQE [@bustin2009]; dMIQE [@huggett2013] | RNA quality, efficiency, reference genes, controls | 16 |
| Enzyme kinetics | STRENDA [@tipton2014] | assay conditions, substrate range, replication | 16 |
| Biocatalysis | biocatalysis reporting guidelines [@gardossi2010] | reaction conditions, enzyme form | 18 |
| Human microbiome | STORMS [@mirzayi2021] | controls, batches, confounders, contamination | 17 |
| Metabolomics | MSI [@sumner2007]; mQACC QA/QC [@kirwan2022] | QC samples, run order, identification levels | 21 |
| Chromatin profiling (ChIP-seq) | ENCODE guidelines [@landt2012] | replicates, controls, depth, concordance | 20 |
| Systematic reviews | PRISMA 2020 [@page2021]; PRISMA-EcoEvo [@odea2021] | search, screening, risk of bias | — |
| Data sharing (all) | FAIR principles [@wilkinson2016] | metadata, identifiers, access, licences | 9, 22 |
